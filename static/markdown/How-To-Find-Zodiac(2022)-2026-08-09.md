Better than [Motor Spirit](/book-Motor-Spirit(2022)) which is kind of pre-course reading for this book - sensible decision not to squoosh the two together I think. I like Kobek best when he's prying appart culural moments as he does here (and also in [Soft And Cuddly](/book-Soft-and-Cuddly(2017))). Whether Kobek has identified Zodiac or not is kind of the beside the point (and I don't feel qualified to judge) the meat here is the portrait of the fanzine scene and sf/fantasy fandom of the 1950's, 60's, and 70s. Fascinating both in an of itself and for the way it prefigures the forms and norms of internet fora.
