Picked this up in the charity shop on Hornsey High Road for £1.50! In hardback no less! 

I always forget how much I enjoy Zadie Smith's novels. For some reason I always think of them as difficult but I don't find them so when I actually get round to reading them. I felt this one kind of faltered a little in  the last third, after the pivot of Bogle's story but I thought up to that point might be my favourite of her books.
