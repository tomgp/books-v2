At first I was like 'this is bad'. And it is. On a sentence by sentence level it's solid no though cliché is left on the shelf. The world building is kind of stupid, [the great foot of nerdism is clomping all over the shop](https://www.toffeemilkshake.co.uk/notes/mjohnharrison-veryafraid#:~:text=It%20is%20the%20great%20clomping%20foot%20of%20nerdism). But if the sentences are weak, at the other end of the spectrum are Sanderson'sterngths; mega scale plot architecture. In the same way the joys of detective fiction are in the minute mechanisms of the plot within the rules of the genere the pleasures of this kind of epic fantasy are like the enjoyment of infrastructure. How he maneuvres his large chunks into place laying the foundations for the next 4000 plus pages is somethign to see. Will I be reading those next 4000 plus pages remains to be seen. There is a temptation to try and blast through the rest of "the stormlight archive" before xmas but I think that migth be too much.
